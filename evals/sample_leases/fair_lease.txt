RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT - ADDITIONAL TERMS

Landlord: Maple Grove Properties Inc.
Tenant: Alex Sample
Rental unit: Unit 504, 88 Example Avenue, Toronto, Ontario
Term: 12 months, starting September 1, 2026

1. Rent. The rent is $2,350 per month and is due on the first day of each month.

2. Rent deposit. The tenant will pay a rent deposit of $2,350, which will be applied to the last month of the tenancy. Interest will be paid on it every year as required by law.

3. Keys. A refundable key deposit of $60 is required for two door keys and one building fob.

4. Utilities. Heat and water are included in the rent. The tenant pays for hydro and internet.

5. Insurance. The tenant must carry tenant insurance with at least $1,000,000 of liability coverage for the whole tenancy.

6. Smoking. Smoking and vaping are not permitted inside the unit or on the balcony.

7. Cleanliness. The tenant will keep the unit in a clean condition.

8. Damage. The tenant will pay to repair any damage caused by the wilful or negligent conduct of the tenant or the tenant's guests.

9. Entry. The landlord will give at least 24 hours written notice before entering, stating the reason, and will only enter between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m., except in an emergency.

10. Locks. The tenant shall not change or add locks without the landlord's written consent.

11. Subletting. The tenant may assign or sublet the unit only with the landlord's written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.

12. Ending the tenancy. To end a monthly tenancy, the tenant gives 60 days written notice ending on the last day of a month.

13. Returned payments. If a payment is returned NSF, the tenant will pay the landlord's bank charges plus a $20 administration charge.
