APARTMENT RENTAL CONTRACT

Landlord: J. Example
Tenant: Sam Placeholder
Premises: Basement apartment, 12 Sample Road, Mississauga, Ontario
Term: one year from May 1, 2026

1. This contract replaces the Ontario Standard Form of Lease.

2. Rent is $1,900 per month, payable on the 1st.

3. At signing, the tenant pays first and last month's rent plus a damage deposit of $1,500.

4. A late fee of $15 per day applies to any rent received after the 1st.

5. The tenant must provide twelve post-dated cheques before moving in.

6. No pets are allowed. A pet found in the unit will lead to immediate eviction.

7. The tenant is responsible for all repairs and maintenance, including appliances and plumbing.

8. The landlord may enter the apartment at any time to inspect it.

9. The landlord may increase the rent every six months.

10. The tenant must move out at the end of the one-year term.

11. Subletting is not permitted under any circumstances.

12. If rent is unpaid for 10 days, the landlord may change the locks and keep the tenant's belongings until rent is paid.

13. A non-refundable lease preparation fee of $300 is due at signing.

14. The tenant waives any rights under the Residential Tenancies Act that conflict with this contract.
